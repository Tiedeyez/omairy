## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

First public release.

- A winged Omarchy mark that lives on your desktop: the Omarchy icon as a
  hollow pixel body, block wings that flap, and a block-art glow whose frames
  echo outward and dissolve through shade textures. Everything takes its
  colour from your theme.
- It flies in long, lazy arcs, floats gently, glides now and then and
  sometimes turns a loop. It visits the window you are working in and perches
  on top of it to rest.
- Click it for something to learn about Omarchy: 126 keybindings and
  commands from the Omarchy manual, shown in a card styled like Omarchy's
  own popups.
- Every few minutes, at random, a small mark or emoticon pops up over its
  head.
- Commands: `omarchy-shell tiedeyez.omairy toggle|show|hide|summon|poke|demo`.
  `demo` runs through everything it can do in about 40 seconds.
- Stays out of your way: only the body takes clicks, it never takes keyboard
  focus, fullscreen windows cover it, and it holds still when animations are
  off.
- Starts no processes, reads and writes no files, makes no network requests.
  See DISCLOSURE.md.
